# Codex Buddy Look Mechanics

Codex Buddy is a compact chibi gym buddy with a blue cap, bold cartoon outline, strong arms, orange star shorts, and workout-state poses.

## Anchors

- Keep the feet/lower body on a stable baseline.
- Preserve the cap shape, strong-arm silhouette, orange shorts, and cartoon outline in every direction.
- Do not add new props, UI, text, symbols, shadows, scenery, speed lines, or detached effects.

## Gaze Motion

- Eyes and eyelids lead the direction.
- Head and upper torso follow with a subtle turn or tilt.
- Arms stay attached and may shift slightly with the torso, but they should not change the pet identity.
- The whole sprite must not rotate as a flat sticker.

## Cardinal Poses

- `000 up`: eyes and head clearly look upward; chin/face angle suggests attention above.
- `090 right`: eyes, face, and head turn toward viewer screen-right; right side of face/body becomes more visible.
- `180 down`: eyes and head clearly look downward; posture compresses slightly without becoming sad/failed.
- `270 left`: eyes, face, and head turn toward viewer screen-left; left side of face/body becomes more visible.

## Continuity

Rows 9 and 10 should form one continuous clockwise look loop. Each 22.5-degree step should move the same parts by a small, even amount.
