# Luffy Look Direction Mechanics

- Preserve the existing chibi pet identity: straw hat, red vest, blue shorts, yellow sash, sandals, compact body, upbeat adventure expression.
- Keep the lower body and feet on a consistent baseline so the frames work inside Codex's fixed pet cell.
- Use eyes, face angle, hat brim, head tilt, and a subtle shoulder turn to show look direction.
- Do not rotate the whole sprite or redraw the outfit into a different costume.
- Keep the silhouette simple and readable at desktop-pet scale.
